Privacy policy — AutoLogist mail

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

What the app accesses

Read-only access (gmail.readonly) to the owner's own Gmail mailbox. Only messages from freight-exchange senders (chat.timocom.com, clicktrans.pl) are processed. Other messages are not opened.

What is stored

From those messages: company name, company ID on the exchange, time and text of the message, and load details (route, dates, weight, size). Personal names, phone numbers and e-mail addresses are removed before storage.

Where and who sees it

On the owner's private server, with encrypted backups. The data is not sold, not shared and not used for advertising, except that hosting and backup providers store it on the owner's behalf.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted at the owner's request.
